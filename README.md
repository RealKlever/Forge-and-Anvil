# Forge-and-Anvil

## Gameplay Tutorial

You will spawn inside the forge, to get started head outside to the stable. There will be a menu there (the store) which you can use to buy items. To start, buy one handle, one iron, and two coal. Head back into the forge and head to the back right of the building. In the corner, there will be three barrels, in each of the barrels, the items you just bought will be sticking out.

First, grab one of the coal, and hover it over the furnace in the middle of the forge. There will be a slot you can insert the coal in, it will be a blue outline of the coal. Once you find it, insert the coal by letting go of the grip button on your controller. This will start the furnace.

Next, grab the piece of iron and drop it anywhere in the furnace. The iron should proceed to heat up, which can be seen by its changing color. Wait for the iron to finish heating, which will be noted by a dinging noise.
    *If the furnace goes out while the iron is heating up, grab the other piece of coal and insert in the coal slot to restart the furnace.

Once the iron is finished heating, take it out of the furnace, and place it onto the anvil. Grab the hammer on the table next to the furnace (the hammer will not match the asthetic of the rest of the forge). To start the hammering process, lift the hammer up, and press the index finger's trigger. Once you press the trigger, bring the hammer down and hit the metal. If the hit was registered, you should hear and hitting sound. Repeat this process for each hit (lift, press trigger, hit metal).
    * **NOTE**: Make sure you bring the hammer up a decent amount, you would not bring the hammer up an inch in a real blacksmith shop to hit metal.
    * To hit the metal, it also must be on the anvil

You know when the metal is done needing to be hit once it transforms into the blade. One you obtain the blade, grab the handle from the barrel. Bring it to the blade, and combine them by placing the handle on top of the blade. Once you do this, the two will combine and transform into a sword.
